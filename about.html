Our Story

ChaloChale — Because the world is too big to stay home.

What started as two friends planning a Goa trip in 2019 is now a travel community of 50,000+ explorers across India and beyond.

How We Started

ChaloChale was born on a chaotic platform at Delhi airport — two friends with a shared love for spontaneous travel and zero patience for overpriced packages.

We believed travel should be simple, honest and joyful. So we built an app that shows you real prices, real stays and real experiences — no hidden fees, no tourist-trap traps.

Today, our team of travel obsessives hand-checks every hotel, maps every itinerary and answers every query personally. Because for us, "chalo chale" isn't a slogan — it's how we live.

Our Journey

2019 — The Beginning

Two friends book their first Goa trip and realise booking platforms are broken. ChaloChale is sketched on a napkin.

2021 — Launch

MVP goes live with 12 Indian destinations. First 500 bookings happen in month one — mostly word of mouth.

2023 — Go International

Bali, Dubai and Europe packages launch. Community crosses 25,000 travellers.

2025 — Award Winner

Named "Best Emerging Travel Platform". Team grows to 40 across product, ops and 24/7 support.

2026 — What's Next

AI-powered trip planner, group bookings and creator collabs — the journey continues. ChaloChale!
